The problem

Job seekers guess at what employers want. This project replaces the guess.

Most job seekers rely on scattered advice about "hot skills" without any real data behind it. Meanwhile, thousands of postings on LinkedIn contain exactly the signal needed — which skills recur, what salaries roles actually pay, and which cities and industries are hiring the most right now.

This project scrapes and analyzes LinkedIn job postings to build a KPI-driven Power BI dashboard that lets a job seeker drill down by role, location, and skill — turning noisy job-market chatter into a concrete, filterable view.

The dataset

Scraped job postings dataset

Postings were collected across 10+ industries and cleaned in Python (Pandas, NumPy) into a structured table of role, company, location, industry, required skills, and posted salary range.

Insights

What the data revealed

01

SQL and Python are the two most frequently requested skills across analyst and BI roles, ahead of any single BI tool.

02

Bengaluru and Gurugram lead hiring volume, consistent with IT services and fintech hubs concentrating in those cities.

03

Salary ranges widen sharply moving from analyst to scientist roles, reflecting the added value of modeling skills.

04

IT services and fintech post the most openings, together accounting for a large share of the analyzed postings.
