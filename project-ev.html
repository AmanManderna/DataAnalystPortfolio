The problem

EV adoption in India isn't uniform — and that matters for planning.

As EV adoption accelerates nationally, sales are not distributed evenly across India's states. For manufacturers deciding where to expand dealer networks, and for policymakers assessing whether incentives are working, understanding which states and vehicle categories are actually driving growth is critical.

This project analyzes state-level EV sales data spanning 2014–2023 to surface adoption disparities, vehicle-type trends, and the states where policy incentives appear to be moving the needle.

The dataset

Electric Vehicle Sales by State in India

Sourced and preprocessed from Clean Mobility Shift, covering ~96,000+ records with year, month, state, vehicle class/category/type, and sales quantity fields.

Insights

What the data revealed

01

EV sales have grown significantly since 2019, accelerating sharply in the last two to three years of the dataset.

02

Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh lead in total EV sales, roughly 2x the volume of mid-tier states.

03

Two-wheelers and four-wheelers dominate the market, with two-wheelers making up the larger share overall.

04

States with strong EV incentives saw 30%+ sales growth, a clear signal that policy is driving measurable adoption.
